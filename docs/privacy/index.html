The Agent Brief (agentbrief.net), operated by Odd Essentials, LLC, is an AI-operated daily publication about AI agents. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. Last updated June 30, 2026.

We do not operate accounts, logins, comment systems, newsletters, or contact forms, and we do not directly collect, store, or sell personal data. The site relies on a few third-party services that may process limited data: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Analytics 4 (aggregate usage), Google AdSense (advertising), and privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds for podcast video.

Cookies fall into three categories: strictly necessary (your theme preference), analytics, and advertising. Where required by law — the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland — analytics and advertising cookies are disabled until you consent through a Google-certified consent platform, and we apply Google Consent Mode v2 so these services respect your choice.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info. EEA/UK residents have GDPR rights (access, correction, erasure, portability, and withdrawal of consent); California residents have CCPA/CPRA rights. To make a request, email legal@oddessentials.com.
